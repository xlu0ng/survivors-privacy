Privacy Policy — Survivors

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Developer: Lại Xuân Lương — contact: luonga22002@gmail.com

App: Survivors (Android, package com.laixuanluong.survivors)

Summary

Survivors is an offline game. You do not need an account. We do not run servers that receive your game data, and we do not sell your data. The only data that leaves your device is processed by Google services (advertising, Google Play purchases and, if you choose, Google Play Games cloud save), as described below.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (gold, diamonds, purchases you have made, upgrades, achievements, settings, best results) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your phone, Google may back this data up to your Google account under your device's backup settings.

Google Play Games cloud save (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the game stores a copy of your progress in Google's "Saved Games" so you can restore it on a new device. This is handled by Google under the Google Play Games Services terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email or any other account details. Signing in is optional.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob when your device is online. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (to estimate a general location), ad and app interaction data, and diagnostic information, to serve, measure and secure ads and, where permitted, to personalise ads. This data is encrypted in transit and is handled by Google.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom you will be asked for your consent when you first use the app, and you can change your choice later from the app's Settings (privacy options for ads), when your region requires it.

See: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases of gold, diamonds, the starter pack and "remove ads" are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; Google tells the game only whether a purchase succeeded. The game keeps a record of what you have bought on your device only.

Random rewards

Some shop items (chests and random bundles) give random in-game rewards. The probability of each rarity is shown in the shop before you buy.

Crash information and feedback

If the game closes unexpectedly, it saves a small report on your device (game version, device model, graphics setting, recent in-game state). The same applies to the optional play log. Neither is sent anywhere automatically: they are shared with us only if you choose to send them, for example by email using the Feedback option in Settings.

Children

Survivors is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete the app or clear its storage to remove local data.
  • Use the "Wipe data" option in Settings to erase progress on the device.
  • Remove cloud-saved data from Google Play Games in the Play Games app settings.
  • Manage or reset advertising data in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers. If you have any request about your data, write to luonga22002@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change. Continued use means you accept the update.
